Terms of Use

The terms for using Hirely, its AI features and its subscriptions.

Last updated October 2026.

The service

Hirely helps you find jobs, track applications and draft cover letters and answers with AI. You review every draft and submit applications yourself on the employer's site.

AI drafts

Drafts are written from the profile and résumé you provide. Read each one before you use it: you are responsible for what you submit, including that it is accurate.

Plans and purchases

Free includes the board, finder, reminders and 1 Auto Apply a week. Plus costs $14.99 per month or $134.99 per year and includes 50 Auto Applies, 100 chat edits and 5 résumé reviews per month. Max costs $29.99 per month or $269.99 per year and includes 150 Auto Applies, 300 chat edits and 15 résumé reviews per month. A pack of 10 extra Auto Applies costs $2.99 and never expires. Prices listed here are in USD; the App Store shows your applicable price before you confirm.

Auto-renewing subscriptions and free trials

Plus and Max are auto-renewing subscriptions for the monthly or annual period you choose. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or after an applicable free trial. Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends, at the price for your selected plan.

Eligible Plus subscribers receive a 7-day free trial. After 7 days, the trial converts to the selected paid Plus subscription ($14.99 per month or $134.99 per year) unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription where applicable.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name (Apple Account / Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Hirely. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current subscription or trial period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or your Hirely account does not cancel your Apple subscription.

Allowances and refunds

Subscriptions renew automatically through your Apple Account until you cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions at least 24 hours before renewal. Monthly allowances reset on the 1st of each month (UTC) and don't carry over. Free Auto Applies reset each Monday (UTC) and may be paused when free usage is high. Packs don't expire. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Invites

When someone you invite signs in and enters your username in their first 14 days, they get 2 bonus Auto Applies. Once they've used their first Auto Apply, you get 2 too, for up to 10 people a month. Bonus Auto Applies don't expire and have no cash value. Invites from fake or duplicate accounts don't count, and we may remove rewards earned that way.

Fair use

Don't use the service to send applications you haven't reviewed, to impersonate someone else, or to get around plan limits. We may suspend accounts that do.

No guarantees

Job listings come from employers' public job boards and may be out of date. We can't promise any application will lead to an interview or offer. The service is provided as is, to the extent the law allows.
