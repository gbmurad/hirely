Frequently asked questions

How does Auto Apply work?

Sign in with Apple and provide your profile and résumé. Auto Apply uses the job posting and your information to draft a tailored cover letter and answers to common questions. You can edit the draft or chat to change its length or tone. Always check accuracy before using it.

Does Hirely submit applications for me?

No. You review every draft and submit the application yourself on the employer’s website. You stay in control of what gets sent.

How does the free trial work, and how do I cancel?

Eligible Plus subscribers can try Plus free for 7 days. After the trial, your selected plan renews at $14.99 per month or $134.99 per year unless canceled. On iPhone, open Settings → your name (Apple Account / Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Hirely. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends or the next renewal. Deleting Hirely or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Use Restore Purchases in Hirely’s purchase screen while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. If your subscription still does not appear, contact support with your plan and a description of the issue. Do not include payment details or your password.

How do I delete my account?

Open Hirely → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and usage history from our server. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone. Manage or cancel your subscription separately in Apple Account subscriptions.

Where is my data?

Your board, saved searches, profile and résumé are stored on your iPhone. We keep account details, plan and purchase information, invite information and usage counts on our server. When you use Auto Apply, the job posting, profile and résumé are sent to our server and Anthropic to produce your draft; we do not store those contents or the draft on our server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

When do my allowances reset?

Free Auto Applies reset every Monday (UTC) and may be paused when free usage is high. Paid monthly allowances reset on the 1st of each month (UTC), including with annual plans, and do not carry over. Purchased extra Auto Applies and invite bonuses do not expire.
