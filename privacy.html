Privacy Policy

Your job search stays on your iPhone. Here’s what we handle to make Hirely work.

Last updated October 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your board (saved jobs, stages, notes, reminders), your saved searches and your profile and résumé are stored on your device. We don't receive or keep copies of them.

What we keep

  • Your account, if you sign in with Apple to use Auto Apply: an identifier from Apple, the email Apple shares (which can be a private relay address) and your name if you choose to share it.
  • Your plan and purchases: which subscription or packs you've bought, as reported by the App Store.
  • Your username and invites, if you set a username for your invite link: the username appears on your invite page, and we record who invited whom so we can give out rewards.
  • Usage counts: how many Auto Applies you've used and when, so we can apply your plan's limits.

Auto Apply

When you tap Auto Apply, the job posting and your profile and résumé are sent to our server and to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the draft. We don't store the posting, your résumé or the draft on our server. Anthropic processes this data to provide the service under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models.

Job listings

The job finder reads public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby) directly from your phone. Company logos are loaded from a public favicon service.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Settings › Delete Account. That removes your account and usage history from our server. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Contact

Hirely is provided by George Murad. For privacy questions, visit Support.

This website
